EMBER YARD

A round-based tactical shooter in a dusk dockyard. You attack with two allies against four defenders. Plant the spike at Site A or Site B and defend it until it blows — or eliminate the whole enemy team. First to 5 rounds takes the yard. Earn credits for kills and round wins, and spend them on better guns between rounds. Buy your gear, then click the yard to lock your aim.
